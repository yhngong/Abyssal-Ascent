Your tether snapped at the bottom of the ocean. Pilot the bathyscaphe NEREUS-VII slowly back to the surface, fight off abyssal leviathans from the bridge, manage your light & motor signatures, and repair hull breaches before the cabin floods and you drown.

CONTROLS & SYSTEMS

  • Mouse + Left Click Aim & Fire Weapon (in Pilot Seat)
  • 1 / 2 or Q / Right-Click Switch Harpoon ↔ Bullets
  • R Reload Autocannon Drum
  • M Toggle Motor ON / OFF (Ascend vs. Silent Stealth)
  • Shift (Hold) Turbine Overdrive (Faster Ascent, Loud!)
  • F Toggle Floodlights ON / OFF
  • C Active Sonar Ping (Reveals Dark, Makes Noise)
  • Space / Tab Leave / Enter Pilot Seat
  • W A S D + E Walk Submarine Cabin & Repair Parts

ABYSSAL THREAT INTEL (7 SPECIES)

  • Blind Trench Stalker: Eyeless! Listens to noise. Turn Motor OFF [M] & hold gunfire so it swims past!
  • Photovore Angler: Drawn to ship lights! Turn Floodlights OFF [F] to make it lose lock!
  • Void Shadow Kraken: Hates bright light! Stalks in darkness — shine Floodlights ON [F] to burn & slow it!
  • Crusher Leviathan: Bulletproof shell! Use Heavy Harpoons [1] to pierce its chitin armor!
  • Razor Viper Swarm: Erratic fast pack! Mow them down with Rapid Bullets [2]!
  • Volt Siphonophore: Bio-EMP jellyfish! Scrambles lights & power if it shocks the hull!
  • Mimic Siren: Pretends to be a calm glowing orb until close — watch its teeth!
